Section 09
Methodology & Data Currency
This portfolio report was generated on 29 March 2026 from the individual supplier assessments of the top ten suppliers by procurement value. Each supplier was assessed across six pillars (Financial Stability 25%, ESG & Sustainability 20%, Regulatory & Legal 20%, Cybersecurity 15%, Operational & Geopolitical Resilience 15%, Reputational Intelligence 5%) using rubric-based scoring derived exclusively from publicly available sources. SaaS/cloud suppliers (Microsoft, DocuSign, OpenAI, Comarch) and critical infrastructure suppliers (Amprion, Siemens, ABB, Schneider IT) use sector-adjusted weighting profiles. All findings are evidence-based and traceable to source URLs. Portfolio score is the unweighted mean of all ten overall scores. Risk distribution thresholds: HIGH <60, MEDIUM 60–79, LOW ≥80. No supplier score reflects Encevo-provided information — all data sourced from public filings, regulatory disclosures, and verified media as of the assessment date. This report does not constitute a legal opinion or financial advice.

SPECTRUM's Supplier Intelligence Platform assesses each supplier across six risk pillars: Financial Health, ESG & Sustainability, Regulatory Compliance, Cyber Security, Operational Resilience, and Reputational Risk. Each pillar is scored 0–100 based on a weighted model combining structured data, public records, regulatory filings, open-source intelligence and proprietary analyst review. Portfolio scores represent a procurement-value-weighted average across all assessed suppliers.

Score Bands: 80–100 = Low Risk (green); 60–79 = Medium Risk (amber); 0–59 = High Risk (red). Data currency: assessments reflect information available as of 29 March 2026. Scores may change materially as new information becomes available.
