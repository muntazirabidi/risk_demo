Recommended Contract Provisions

📋 Clause 7.3: Data Residency & Sovereignty
"Vendor shall ensure that all Customer Data, including backups and disaster recovery copies, remains stored exclusively within [SPECIFY: EU Region / US Region / Customer-Designated Geography] at all times during the Term. Vendor shall not transfer, access, or process Customer Data outside the designated geography without Customer's prior written consent. For government or highly regulated deployments, Customer may require on-premises or dedicated cloud instance deployment with contractually guaranteed data isolation."
Rationale: Palantir operates globally. For EU customers subject to GDPR or financial institutions with data localization requirements, explicit geographic restrictions are critical. Palantir can accommodate these requirements—ensure contract specificity.
🔒 Clause 12.1: Security Incident Notification
"Vendor shall notify Customer within four (4) hours of becoming aware of any Security Incident that affects or may reasonably affect Customer Data. Notification shall include: (a) description of the incident, (b) affected data categories, (c) preliminary impact assessment, (d) immediate remediation steps taken. Vendor shall provide detailed forensic report within seventy-two (72) hours and cooperate fully with Customer's incident response procedures."
Rationale: While Palantir has strong security posture (FedRAMP, IL5), mission-critical deployments require rapid incident disclosure. Standard 72-hour notification windows are insufficient for defense/intelligence use cases.
⚖️ Clause 15.4: Service Level Agreements (SLAs)
"Platform Availability: Vendor guarantees 99.95% uptime measured monthly for Production Environment (excludes scheduled maintenance with 7-day notice). For Critical Government deployments, uptime SLA shall be 99.99%. Remedies: • 99.90-99.95%: 10% monthly service credit • 99.50-99.90%: 25% monthly service credit • <99.50%: 50% monthly service credit + right to terminate for cause without penalty Support Response Times (P1 Critical Issues): • Initial Response: <30 minutes • Vendor Engineer Engaged: <2 hours • Root Cause Analysis: <24 hours"
Rationale: Palantir's platform criticality varies by use case. Command & control systems require stricter SLAs than analytics environments. Negotiate tiered SLAs matching your criticality.
🔄 Clause 19.2: Professional Services Escalation
"Vendor acknowledges that certain deployments require senior technical resources. Customer may request escalation to: (a) Named Solutions Architect within vendor organization, (b) Product Engineering team for feature customization discussions, (c) Executive sponsor for strategic alignment. Vendor shall respond to escalation requests within one (1) business day and assign requested resources within three (3) business days for Tier 1 customers."
Rationale: Palantir's implementation complexity varies significantly. Large enterprises and government agencies should secure escalation paths contractually to avoid being stuck with junior resources during critical deployments.
🚪 Clause 22.1: Exit Assistance & Data Portability
"Upon termination or expiration of Agreement, Vendor shall provide Exit Assistance for up to ninety (90) days at no additional charge, including: (a) Extraction of all Customer Data in industry-standard formats (CSV, JSON, Parquet) (b) Documentation of data schemas, ontologies, and pipelines created during engagement (c) Knowledge transfer sessions (minimum 40 hours) to Customer or Customer's designated successor vendor (d) Continued platform access (read-only) for thirty (30) days post-termination Vendor shall not implement technical barriers to data export or charge 'data egress fees' exceeding $X per GB."
Rationale: Palantir's platform creates significant data lock-in through proprietary ontologies and pipelines. Explicit exit rights prevent vendor hold-up problems during renewal negotiations or vendor switches.
