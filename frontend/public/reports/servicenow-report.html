Overall Recommendation

Risk Classification: MEDIUM

TechVendor Solutions demonstrates strong technical capabilities, reputable backing, and solid compliance practices. However, significant financial headwinds, customer concentration risk, and ongoing litigation create meaningful uncertainties.

Recommended Actions:

  • Proceed with enhanced due diligence: Request detailed financial statements, customer references, and updates on CTO litigation
  • Contract protections: Include termination for convenience clause, escrow requirements for critical IP/code, and financial health covenants
  • Regular monitoring: Quarterly reviews of financial health indicators and litigation status for the next 18 months
  • Contingency planning: Develop vendor transition plan in case of financial distress or service disruption
  • Start with pilot: Consider smaller initial engagement to validate service quality before committing to larger contracts

Not recommended if: Your use case requires mission-critical services with no backup options, or if your organization has low risk tolerance for potential vendor instability.
