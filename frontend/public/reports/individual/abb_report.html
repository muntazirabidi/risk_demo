Section 01

Executive Summary

CONDITIONAL

MEDIUM

ABB Ltd is financially strong (S&P: A/Stable, Moody's: A2/Stable; record FY2025 revenues of $33.2B; near-zero net leverage) and demonstrates sector-leading ESG practices (CDP A, SBTi-validated net-zero targets). However, two material risk factors require contractual mitigation: (1) ABB is the world's first triple FCPA recidivist, having resolved a $462.5M coordinated bribery case in December 2022 — the pattern of targeting state-owned energy utilities directly parallels Encevo's counterparty profile; (2) an active US Congressional investigation into ABB's supply of components to Chinese state-owned crane manufacturer ZPMC, installed at US critical infrastructure ports, creates escalating geopolitical and reputational risk. The May 2023 Black Basta ransomware breach with confirmed data exfiltration further elevates cybersecurity concerns. Engagement is recommended with enhanced contract protections.

Primary Recommendation

Engage with enhanced contract protections. Priority actions: (1) obtain DPA closure confirmation; (2) insert FCPA compliance clause; (3) confirm product division scope to address Robotics divestiture risk; (4) request post-breach cybersecurity remediation report.

CONDITIONS FOR QUALIFICATION

  • ABB must provide written confirmation that the December 2022 DOJ Deferred Prosecution Agreement has been formally closed without new charges, and that the enhanced internal compliance programme mandated under the DPA remains in operation.
  • Contract must include robust FCPA/anti-bribery representation and warranty clause, requiring ABB to notify Encevo within 30 days of any new anti-bribery investigation, charge, or enforcement action.
  • Encevo procurement team must confirm that its ABB product/service relationship falls within the Electrification, Motion, or Process Automation divisions — not the Robotics & Discrete Automation business being divested to SoftBank (closing mid-to-late 2026).
  • ABB must provide post-incident cybersecurity remediation report confirming scope of May 2023 breach, whether any Encevo-related data was affected, and security improvements implemented.
  • Annual anti-bribery and FCPA compliance confirmation to be included in supplier review cadence, with right to terminate for breach.
  • Monitor for any US executive orders, procurement restrictions, or regulatory actions arising from the ZPMC Congressional investigation — any escalation to formal enforcement warrants immediate review of ABB relationship.
