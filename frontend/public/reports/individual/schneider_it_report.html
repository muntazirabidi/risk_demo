Section 01

Executive Summary

CONDITIONAL

MEDIUM

Schneider Electric IT Corporation (APC brand) scores 73/100 overall under the critical infrastructure weighting scheme. The group presents exceptional financial stability (A2/A rated, record FY2024 revenues, strong FCF) and best-in-class ESG credentials. However, three ransomware incidents within 18 months — including a November 2024 breach of its developer platform that resulted in stolen data published on the dark web — drive cybersecurity to CRITICAL (37/100). Two active regulatory enforcement actions (€207M French antitrust fine under appeal; $11M US DOJ False Claims Act settlement) further elevate regulatory/legal risk. Engagement should continue subject to cybersecurity remediation assurance and contractual protections.

Primary Recommendation

Proceed with engagement subject to receipt of a cybersecurity remediation plan and NIS2 compliance assurance within 60 days. Impose enhanced monitoring KRIs for cybersecurity incidents and regulatory developments.

CONDITIONS FOR QUALIFICATION

  • Obtain written cybersecurity remediation plan from Schneider Electric addressing the three ransomware incidents, including independent security audit results and timeline for addressing systemic Jira/developer platform vulnerabilities
  • Confirm NIS2 supply chain compliance documentation and any applicable security obligations flow-down clauses
  • Monitor French competition authority appeal outcome (€207M fine); escalate if appeal fails and fine becomes final
  • Request confirmation that the DOJ False Claims Act settlement is fully resolved with no continuing obligations affecting government contract eligibility
  • Annual cybersecurity review of APC/EcoStruxure platform certifications and incident history before contract renewal

Cybersecurity & Data

37/100

CRITICAL
Cybersecurity is the dominant risk in this assessment. Schneider Electric suffered three confirmed ransomware incidents within 18 months (June 2023, January 2024, November 2024), each involving different threat actors and attack vectors, suggesting systemic vulnerabilities rather than isolated incidents. The November 2024 Hellcat breach specifically compromised the Atlassian Jira developer platform, exposing 75,000 unique email addresses, full names of employees and customers, and internal project data — which was subsequently published on the dark web. Multiple CISA ICS advisories against Schneider Electric and APC products compound the exposure. The ISO 27001:2022 certification (valid from January 2025) and transparent public disclosure of incidents are positive signals, but the pattern of repeat breaches in core infrastructure platforms warrants a CRITICAL rating for this pillar. Encevo should request an immediate cybersecurity assurance review before extending or renewing contracts.
