Executive Summary

CONDITIONAL

MEDIUM

Microsoft Corporation scores 62/100 (MEDIUM risk — CONDITIONAL). Engagement is recommended conditional on Encevo securing specific contractual protections to address the four primary risk vectors identified: (1) CLOUD Act / EU data sovereignty — require Microsoft Cloud for Sovereignty deployment with NIS2-compliant provisions; (2) Cybersecurity posture — require SFI progress reporting and CSRB remediation evidence; (3) GDPR compliance — require updated Data Processing Agreement with enhanced EU data handling terms; (4) Azure service continuity — require SLA enhancements with financial penalties for outages exceeding 4 hours. Despite a HIGH/CRITICAL cybersecurity pillar score (39), Microsoft's exceptional financial strength (P1: 88), global scale, and irreplaceable position as an enterprise platform make strategic disengagement impractical. The risk is manageable through contract and monitoring, not avoidance.

Primary Recommendation

Qualify Microsoft Corporation as a CONDITIONAL supplier for cloud and software services to Encevo S.A. The eight conditions above must be implemented as contractual and operational prerequisites. Microsoft's financial solidity and platform irreplaceability mean conditions are manageable, but the CLOUD Act exposure, Cybersecurity pillar score of 39 (CRITICAL band), and pattern of Azure outages require immediate contractual action. Annual score refresh recommended with focus on CSRB remediation status, CLOUD Act legal developments, and GDPR enforcement outcomes.

CONDITIONS FOR QUALIFICATION

  • Condition 1 — Data Sovereignty: Require Microsoft Cloud for Sovereignty deployment for all Encevo workloads — with EU data residency enforcement and NIS2-compliant Supply Chain Security Agreement as a binding contract annex.
  • Condition 2 — GDPR Compliance: Require updated Data Processing Agreement post-LinkedIn GDPR fine (€310M, October 2024) with explicit lawful basis documentation, diagnostic data opt-out, and Schrems II-compliant transfer mechanisms.
  • Condition 3 — DORA Provisions: Require DORA ICT Third-Party Service Provider contractual provisions if Encevo operations fall within EU financial sector scope.
  • Condition 4 — Security Assurance: Require Microsoft SFI (Secure Future Initiative) annual progress reporting and written evidence of CSRB remediation completion — as an ongoing contract condition.
  • Condition 5 — Service Continuity: Require enhanced Azure SLA with financial penalty clauses for outages exceeding 4 hours (>1 occurrence per 12-month period) and quarterly outage trend reporting.
  • Condition 6 — Teams Pricing Remedy: Confirm Encevo benefits from EC September 2025 competition remedy — ensure Teams-free Microsoft 365 pricing option is available and documented in contract.
  • Condition 7 — AI Act Readiness: Before deploying Microsoft Copilot or Azure AI services, require written confirmation of EU AI Act compliance readiness (full application August 2, 2026).
  • Condition 8 — Patch Management: Establish Encevo internal patch management SLA aligned to Microsoft Patch Tuesday — critical patches applied within 30 days; CISA KEV alerts subscribed to.
