MEDIUM

Félix Giorgetti Sàrl scores 67/100, placing it in the CONDITIONAL band (60-79). The company's strongest pillars are Regulatory & Legal (100) and Reputational Intelligence (100), reflecting a clean sanctions record, strong ISO certifications, and a positive public profile. The CONDITIONAL verdict is driven primarily by two gaps: (1) Financial Stability (44/100) — a structurally constrained score reflecting the absence of any publicly verifiable accounts for a private Luxembourg Sàrl rather than evidence of financial distress, with sector-level construction crisis exposure materialising in 2024 [SRC-004]; and (2) ESG & Sustainability (51/100) — the company lacks a published sustainability report, disclosed emissions data, or science-based climate targets, though CSRD preparation is actively underway [SRC-014].
