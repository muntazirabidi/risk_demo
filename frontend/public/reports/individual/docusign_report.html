Section 01

Executive Summary

CONDITIONAL

MEDIUM

DocuSign scores 79/100 under the SaaS/data-processor weight adjustment, placing it at the upper boundary of CONDITIONAL. The company presents strong financial health (debt-free, $920M free cash flow), clean sanctions and regulatory records, and industry-leading security certifications (ISO 27001:2022, SOC 2 Type II). The two material risks are: (1) US CLOUD Act exposure creating an irresolvable data sovereignty conflict for EU client Encevo S.A. — DocuSign's EU data residency options do not eliminate US government compelled-access rights under 18 U.S.C. §2713; and (2) a documented pattern of service outages averaging 13+ incidents per month, including a 7-hour 42-minute outage on March 10, 2026. These risks do not preclude engagement but require specific contractual protections before contract execution.

Primary Recommendation

Proceed with engagement subject to conditions above. Prioritise negotiating CLOUD Act notification clause and 99.9% uptime SLA with financial penalty provisions before contract execution. Assign CISO and Legal review within 30 days.

CONDITIONS FOR QUALIFICATION

  • GDPR Article 28 DPA with explicit CLOUD Act notification clause: Vendor must notify Encevo within 24 hours of any US government compelled-access request relating to Encevo data, to the maximum extent permitted by law.
  • EU data residency clause: All Encevo contract data must remain within EU/EEA at all times. DocuSign to confirm in writing which data centres store Encevo data and certify EU residency at each contract renewal.
  • Service Level Agreement: Uptime SLA minimum 99.9% (monthly), with financial remedy for outages exceeding 4 hours. Current status-page evidence suggests DocuSign does not meet this threshold without contractual enforcement.
  • Incident response clause: DocuSign must notify Encevo within 4 hours of any security incident potentially affecting Encevo data, consistent with GDPR Article 33 processor obligations.
  • Annual security review: DocuSign must provide current SOC 2 Type II report and ISO 27001 certificate to Encevo annually, or upon request within 30 days.
