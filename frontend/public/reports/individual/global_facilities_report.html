Section 01

Executive Summary

CONDITIONAL

MEDIUM

Global Facilities S.A. scores 60.3/100 overall. The company has a 25-year operational track record, clean regulatory and legal record (P3: 92/100), strong reputational standing (P6: 100/100), and no adverse findings across most pillars. The primary risk factors are: (1) near-complete financial opacity as a private subsidiary (P1: 39/100 — data gap, not distress), (2) absence of ISO 27001 given physical access to NIS2 critical infrastructure (P4: 55/100), (3) indirect Chinese SOE ownership creating geopolitical sensitivity, and (4) NIS2 supply chain security obligations not publicly confirmed. A critical structural note: Global Facilities is an Encevo-owned subsidiary — the due diligence effectively assesses an intra-group entity.

Primary Recommendation

Retain Global Facilities as intra-group FM service provider subject to implementation of ISO 27001 and formal NIS2 supply chain security assessment. Given the entity is wholly owned by Encevo, the primary governance action is to ensure Encevo Group's internal governance adequately addresses the Chinese SOE ownership risk at board level.

CONDITIONS FOR QUALIFICATION

  • Obtain ISO 27001 certification or equivalent for information security management — required given physical access to Encevo/Creos NIS2 critical infrastructure
  • Confirm NIS2 Article 21 supply chain security obligations have been assessed and documented by Encevo as the essential entity parent
  • Address Chinese SOE ownership transparency — document whether China Southern Power Grid International has board-level influence at Encevo that could affect strategic decisions
  • Encevo procurement team to confirm transfer pricing and intra-group service agreement governance for Global Facilities contracts
  • Request standalone financial accounts or parent guarantee confirming ongoing financial support
