Section 01

Executive Summary

CONDITIONAL

MEDIUM

Amprion GmbH is a financially sound, ESG-leading, and reputationally strong German TSO with an overall score of 76/100. The CONDITIONAL verdict reflects two specific structural concerns rather than adverse findings: (1) elevated financial leverage (net debt/EBITDA ~6.9x) driven by a massive €36.4B grid investment programme — structurally expected for a regulated TSO but warrants monitoring via Moody's Baa1 negative outlook tracking; and (2) unverified cybersecurity certification posture despite KRITIS designation, creating an opacity gap that Encevo should resolve through direct contractual disclosure requirements. All sanctions, AML, GDPR, and ESG checks returned clean.

Primary Recommendation

Proceed with engagement subject to contractual inclusion of KRITIS/NIS2 incident notification clause and formal request for BSI §8a security attestation documentation.

CONDITIONS FOR QUALIFICATION

  • Encevo should formally request evidence of Amprion's BSIG §8a IT security attestation (BSI-certified KRITIS compliance) within 90 days of contract renewal
  • Moody's Baa1 rating outlook (currently negative) should be monitored quarterly — a downgrade to Baa2 negative should trigger a formal re-assessment
  • Contract should include NIS2/KRITIS Dachgesetz flow-down clause requiring Amprion to notify Encevo within 72 hours of any cyber incident materially affecting transmission operations
  • Net debt/EBITDA ratio should be tracked annually against the €36.4B capex programme; if ratio exceeds 8x without proportionate RAB growth, flag for financial review
