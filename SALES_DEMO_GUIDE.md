## Recommended Demo Flow (15-20 minutes)

### **Act 1: The Problem (2 min)**
Start by acknowledging their pain points:

> "Most procurement teams spend 90+ days and $15-20K per vendor assessment. Data is scattered across emails, PDFs, and spreadsheets. By the time you finish an assessment, the vendor's situation may have already changed. Does this sound familiar?"

### **Act 2: The Solution - Portfolio Page (10 min)**

**Navigate to: http://localhost:5173/portfolio**

**Step 1: Value Proposition (1 min)**
> "Spectrum transforms that 90-day process into 24-48 hours. Here's what you receive..."

**Step 2: Sample Reports (4 min)**
Scroll to "Sample Intelligence Reports" section:

> "These three reports showcase what we deliver. Let me show you one..."

**Click on any featured report (e.g., Palantir)**

Walk through the full HTML report:
- Overview metrics and risk score
- Five pillars of analysis (Financial, ESG, Human Rights, Sanctions, Cyber)
- Evidence-based findings with sources
- Scroll to key differentiators:
  - **CAPA Recommendations**: "Not just scores - actionable steps with owners and deadlines"
  - **Contract Playbook**: "Risk-based contract clauses tailored to this vendor"

**Step 3: How It Works (2 min)**
Back to Portfolio page, scroll to "How Spectrum Works":

> "The process is simple:
> 1. You send us vendor names via email or spreadsheet
> 2. Our AI autonomously gathers and analyzes data
> 3. You receive comprehensive reports in 24-48 hours
>
> No platform training needed. No software to install. Just send names, get reports."

**Step 4: Active Portfolio (3 min)**
Scroll to portfolio table:

> "Once assessed, vendors move into continuous monitoring. If anything changes - sanctions hit, financial distress, ESG incident - you're alerted immediately, not in your next quarterly review."

- Show filters (Qualified, Conditional, Monitoring)
- Point out CAPA tracking
- Click on any sample vendor to show detail view

### **Act 3: Live Capability Demo - Optional (5 min)**

**Navigate to: http://localhost:5173/**

> "Want to see the AI in action? Watch what happens when we analyze a vendor live..."

- Enter a well-known company (TSMC, Siemens, Johnson & Johnson)

**Scroll down to Before/After comparison**:
> "Here's why teams switch to Spectrum..."

Point to specific ROI elements:
- Time savings: 90+ days → 24-48 hours
- Cost reduction
- Continuous monitoring vs point-in-time snapshots

## Key Talking Points

### **Positioning:**
- **What we sell**: Comprehensive vendor intelligence reports
- **What we're NOT**: A platform you need to learn or software you need to install
- **Business model**: Reports-as-a-Service → Platform access as you scale

### **Differentiation:**
1. **Beyond Scores**: We deliver strategy - CAPA plans, contract recommendations
2. **Evidence-Based**: Every finding linked to verifiable sources for audit defense
3. **Comprehensive**: 5 critical risk pillars in every report
4. **Turnkey**: No integration, no training, no change management
5. **Scalable**: Start with 5 vendors, scale to 500

### **Handle Common Objections:**

**"We already use [competitor]"**
> "Great! Most of our clients started with traditional tools. The difference is depth - we're not replacing questionnaires, we're augmenting with autonomous intelligence gathering and forensic analysis."

**"Do we get platform access?"**
> "Phase 1 is report delivery - we optimize your process without any change management. As volume grows, we can provide portal access. But most clients prefer starting with reports to prove value first."

**"What about our existing vendors?"**
> "We can backfill your current portfolio. Send us your vendor list, we'll deliver initial assessments, then provide continuous monitoring going forward."

**"How accurate is the AI?"**
> "Let me show you..." [Click any full report, show evidence links and sources]

## Tips for Success

1. **Lead with value, not features**: Focus on "90 days → 24 hours" not "multi-agent AI"
2. **Show, don't tell**: Click into reports, let them see the depth
3. **Make it relatable**: Use vendors they recognize
4. **Address the business model clearly**: Reports first, platform later
5. **End with action**: "Which 5 vendors should we start with?"

**Remember**: You're selling peace of mind and time savings, not software.
